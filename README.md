## Background & Overview

I set out to conduct research on the effects that social media has on us as a society and searched for a means to understand what about social media causes damage as well as the characteristics that leave us susceptible to being negatively influenced by it.
This dataset covers countries all over the would with people ranging in age from 13-65, and a range of professions.

## Executive Summary

One of the questions I investigated is whether we could predict whether or not a user was more likely to make, or not make, a purchase, and I found that there were a few factors that seemed to have an influence on users in this regard.
One of these factors was their primary purpose for their use of social media. People who primarily used social media for “News & Updates”, “Learning” and “Business/ Marketing had 1-2% higher average of people who did not make purchases on social media in comparison to people who used social media primarily for “Socialising”, “Networking” and “Entertainment”. I believe this to be because those in the former group are all using social media as a form to improve themselves in some regard and are therefore less likely to make unnecessary purchases on social media. Contrastingly, the latter group are primarily using social media for fun, which would make them more likely to want to purchase things that they see.
